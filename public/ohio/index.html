Zoning in Ohio is carried out by cities and counties under the Ohio Revised Code Chapter 713 (municipal zoning) and Chapter 519 (township zoning) (Ohio Rev. Code Ch. 713 (municipalities); Ch. 519, §§ 519.02-519.25 (townships); Ch. 303 (counties)). Ohio has no single statewide zoning code; land use is delegated to local governments. Municipal corporations zone under ORC Chapter 713 via a planning commission; townships zone under ORC Chapter 519; counties may adopt zoning under ORC Chapter 303. Home-rule municipalities also derive zoning power from Article XVIII of the Ohio Constitution. Each jurisdiction adopts its own ordinance, districts, and standards.

📐 Zoning Variance Appeal Letter in Ohio

Accessory dwelling units (ADUs): Local only — no statewide ADU preemption as of July 2026. Authority over ADUs rests with individual municipalities and townships. Cincinnati legalized ADUs via Ordinance 0266-2023 (first Ohio city), and Columbus voted in Nov. 2025 to permit ADUs by-right in all residential districts. Most Ohio jurisdictions still restrict them.

Home businesses: Local only — Ohio has no statewide home-occupation statute. Home-based business standards are set by each municipal zoning ordinance or township zoning resolution.
